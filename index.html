    Help and keyboard keys

    The tutor never moves for you. You make every move; it watches, warns and explains.

    On the board, tap one of your pieces, then tap where it should go. Or type or dictate a move in Your move.

    Keys with a keyboard, when you are not typing in a box: T threat check, W where are we, H hint, C what changed, F full board, D square details, R repeat, Escape stop speech, Y story, M review mistakes, K my tactics, L opponent's tactics, P my plans, O opponent's plans, B bookmark, S start or end study, U take back. Walk the board: Q turn left, E turn right, I step, J pick up or put down, X cancel, N recentre, V where am I standing. G go to the board, slash to type a move, 1 2 3 coach level. On the board, arrow keys move between squares and Enter or Space selects.

    The engine is small and runs on the phone. Its opinions come from a short search plus rules of thumb, so the tutor says "the engine thinks", not "this is certain".
